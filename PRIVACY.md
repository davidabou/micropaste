# Privacy policy

Micropaste is local-first. There is no account, no cloud and no telemetry: the author of Micropaste receives no data from the app.

## What stays on your computer

Everything you copy (text, links, images, files and their thumbnails), your collections and your settings are stored only on your computer, in `%APPDATA%\com.abou.micropaste`. Text in images is recognized by Windows itself, on your computer. Uninstalling Micropaste lets you delete this folder.

## What goes over the network

Micropaste only connects to the internet for two things:

- **Link previews.** When you copy a link, Micropaste loads the page from its site to show its title, image and icon. That site receives the request like any visit from a browser: the address of the page and your IP address. Turn off **Link previews** in the settings and Micropaste never contacts these sites.
- **Updates.** Micropaste checks [GitHub](https://github.com/davidabou/micropaste/releases) for a new version at startup and every 6 hours, and downloads it from there. GitHub receives your IP address and the version you use, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The Microsoft Store and the GitHub releases, where you download Micropaste, have their own privacy policies.
